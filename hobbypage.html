Meshtastic

Meshtastic for those who don't know is a free open source LoRA radio mesh network project its become a large success. It's very versatile it can be used for IOT applications,messaging or asset tracking and much more. Some great things abotu meshtastic is that it is a mesh network meaning the more nodes the stronger the network is and with it you can go Extremely far in my experience with a well placed repeater or some good elevation its posssible to get over 300km!
